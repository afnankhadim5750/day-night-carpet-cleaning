Services

Cleaning services

Every clean is quoted up front based on the size and condition of the job — no surprises. Call +1 403-540-5564 or request a quote online.

Carpet Deep Cleaning

Our hot-water extraction process reaches deep into the pile to lift dirt, dust and allergens that vacuuming leaves behind, leaving carpets brighter and softer underfoot.

  • Pre-treatment for traffic lanes and spots
  • Deodorizing included on request
  • Fast drying thanks to powerful extraction

Upholstery Cleaning

Sofas and chairs collect body oils, crumbs and pet hair. We match the method to your fabric — from durable synthetics to delicate weaves — and refresh the whole piece evenly.

  • Sofas, loveseats, armchairs & dining chairs
  • Fabric-tested solutions, safe on colours
  • Cushions cleaned on all sides

Stairs & Hallways

The hardest-working carpet in the house. We detail every tread and riser by hand where the big machine can't reach, restoring the pile in your highest-traffic areas.

  • Hand-detailing on edges and corners
  • Ideal add-on to a full-home clean

Area Rug Cleaning

Patterned, wool and synthetic rugs get a gentle but thorough clean that protects dyes and fibres while lifting out the grit that wears rugs down from the inside.

  • Colour-safe process, tested first
  • Fringe cleaning and grooming included
